## 1. Wandr is not a safety device

**Read this section even if you skip the rest.**

Wandr shows you where your friends' radios last reported themselves to be. It depends on consumer GPS, long-range radio links, battery-powered hardware, and open mesh networking. Any of these can fail, and at a festival they frequently will. Positions may be minutes or hours old, wildly inaccurate, or absent entirely, and a radio can appear connected while sending nothing useful.

Do not rely on Wandr for:

- emergencies of any kind;
- medical, rescue, or lost-person response;
- navigation in terrain where a wrong direction is dangerous;
- keeping track of children or anyone who cannot look after themselves;
- any situation where being wrong about someone's location could cause harm.

In an emergency, call your local emergency number or find festival medical staff. Wandr is a convenience for finding friends in a crowd. Treat it as a hint, never as a fact.
