# Support

Email **wandr.festival@gmail.com** and include the model of your phone and radio.

Common fixes:

- **Radio won't connect**: if the Meshtastic app is open and connected to the radio, disconnect it there first. Radios accept one app at a time.
- **Friends' positions are old**: a radio only sends after moving 25 m or at its heartbeat. Tap the person and use *Ping for position* for a fresh fix.
- **App stops working in your pocket (Android)**: in the phone's settings, set Wandr's battery usage to *Unrestricted*.
